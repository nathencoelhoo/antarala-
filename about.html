A THRESHOLD BETWEEN THE BODY AND THE WORLD /

THE NAME /

In Indian temple architecture, the antarāla is the passage between the open hall and the inner sanctum. It is a space you cross slowly, from outside to inside.

We make clothes in the same spirit. Each piece is built to stand between you and the heat, the crowd and the day, and to feel calm to wear.

THE FOUNDER /

[FOUNDER NAME] is a business student and the founder of ANTARĀLA. The brand began as a commitment to three things: meticulous craftsmanship, operational transparency and systemic quality control.

Every garment is traced from loom to fitting, checked at each stage, and carries the name of the weaver who made the cloth. The aim is a label that is run as carefully as it is cut.

THE STUDIO /

Our base is in Goa. The cloth comes from the weavers of Assam and Bhagalpur. Each piece is named for a term from Indian architecture.

HOW WE WORK /

NO LOGOS
The silhouette and the Spine seam are the signature. Nothing is printed on the surface.

MADE TO MEASURE
Every garment is cut after a fitting, in small batches, and delivered in 4 to 6 weeks.

NAMED CLOTH
Each garment carries a serial number, the weaver's name and the loom's location.

BUILT FOR HEAT
Structured cloths that hold a shape without trapping heat, chosen for Indian summers.

FINISHED INSIDE
Closed French seams, so the inside is as clean as the outside.

NO HARDWARE
Where a garment can close without a zip or snap, it does.
